# studio 707 — how to use this

*you found the gig bag. here's everything inside.*

---

## first — turn it on

When you open the link, you'll see a black screen with a little red **power** button.

Tap it. That's the amp warming up. Then the guitar appears.

*(Browsers need you to tap something before sound works — that's why the power button is there.)*

---

## the guitar

**Swipe your finger across the strings** — anywhere from the neck down to the body. Each string sounds different. Drag slowly or fast, however you feel.

That's it. No rules.

---

## the stickers

There are three stickers on the guitar body. Tap whichever one matches your mood:

| Sticker | When to tap |
|---------|-------------|
| **NANA** (pink) | You need to vent. Black Stones energy. |
| **NewJeans** (bunny) | You're feeling shy or want hype. Send vibes. |
| **Deftones** (horse) | Late-night chill mood. White Pony hours. |

They glow when you tap them. That's you sending a signal — no words needed.

---

## setlist — drop a link

At the bottom, tap **setlist → drop a link**.

Paste a **YouTube** link and hit **tape it ✎**.

- The song plays for both of you **at the same time** (jam sync)
- When you drop a link, he gets a ping — open the site and you'll be on the same beat
- Tap ▶ on any past YouTube link to replay it together
- Spotify links play solo (YouTube only for sync)

Look for the green **jam sync live** bar at the bottom after you close the setlist.

---

## pager — the back door

At the bottom, tap **pager → dial in**.

1. Tap the numbers on the keypad
2. Enter the code: **707**
3. Hit **send**

If it's right, the screen turns green — **CONNECTED_**

You'll see a message waiting for you. Below that, you can type whatever's on your mind and hit **transmit →**

That's the direct line. No judgment, no pressure.

---

## closing things

Tap **✕** or tap outside any popup to go back to the guitar.

Everything stays on one screen — nothing reloads, nothing gets lost mid-vibe.

---

## that's literally it

| You want to… | Do this |
|--------------|---------|
| Make a sound | Swipe the strings |
| Send a mood | Tap a sticker |
| Share a song | Setlist → paste link |
| Say something real | Pager → 707 → transmit |
